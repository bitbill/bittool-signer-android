# Final audit (read-only, before release)

Eight independent finders (secrets lifecycle, Android platform surface, crypto and engine host, iOS parity of setup / wallets / sign / activity-settings-shell, robustness and performance). Every finding was then checked by skeptical verifiers trying to refute it (two for P0/P1, one otherwise); only findings they could not refute are listed as confirmed.

Result: 18 findings, 14 confirmed, 4 rejected. No P0.
